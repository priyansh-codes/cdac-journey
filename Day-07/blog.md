### Morning Sessions: C++ Deep Dive

Our morning started with a **C++ Lab**, where we were given time to catch up on any unsolved problems. After the lab, we had a C++ theory session with Yogita ma'am. She began with a recap of **structures** before moving on to some more advanced concepts.

We learned how to access a variable within a struct using the **arrow operator (`->`)**, and how to pass structures to functions. Ma'am also explained the difference between a **shallow copy** and a **deep copy**, demonstrating how to use `strcpy` for string manipulation within a struct.

The lecture then shifted to **unions**, with a clear explanation of how they differ from structs. This was followed by a dive into **dynamic memory allocation** using three key functions: `malloc`, `free`, and `calloc`. We also covered the **dereferencing operator (`*`)**, the **address operator (`&`)**, and the **null pointer**.

### Afternoon: JavaScript Lab & Array Mastery

After lunch, our afternoon session began with a **JavaScript theory** class with Anu ma'am. She started with a comprehensive look at **arrays**. We learned about the different functions used to manipulate arrays, including:
- **Adding & removing elements**: `push`, `pop`, `unshift`, `shift`
- **Searching & checking**: `includes`, `indexOf`, `lastIndexOf`, `findIndex`
- **Modifying**: `splice`, `concat`
- **Sorting & reversing**: `sort`, `reverse`
- **Transforming**: `map`, `filter`, `reduce`, `flat`
- **Joining & converting**: `join`, `toString`, `concat`, `slice`
- **Iterating**: `forEach`, `every`, `some`

Ma'am explained each function's purpose and demonstrated its use. It was a dense but incredibly useful session on array methods.

The day was supposed to end with a **JavaScript Lab**, but I decided to head home. The lab was focused on practicing earlier assignments, which I had already completed during my self-study sessions. I felt I could make better use of my time by continuing to learn and practice at my own pace.
