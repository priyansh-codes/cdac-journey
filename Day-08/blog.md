**Date:** 3rd September 2025

### Morning Sessions: A C++ Problem-Solving Session

My morning began with a **C++ lab** where we worked on completing any pending assignments. This was followed by a **C++ theory** session with Yogita ma'am, where she focused on the relationship between **pointers and arrays**.

She gave us a fun problem-solving task: to create an array dynamically using a pointer, store values in it, and then display the contents. This hands-on exercise was a great way to put theory into practice. She then covered iterating through arrays and explained the concept of a **string as an array**. The session also touched on **pointers to pointers** and the **dynamic allocation of 2D arrays**, which is a crucial skill. We finished up with a discussion on implementing an **array of strings**.

### Afternoon: JavaScript Functions and Events

After a lunch break, the afternoon session was dedicated to **JavaScript** with Anu ma'am. She started with a quick recap of functions before diving into more advanced topics. We learned about **function constructors** and the concept of **call by value** in JavaScript.

She then explained **local and global variables**, as well as **nested functions**. We also learned about **default values for function parameters** and the fact that there is **no function overloading in JavaScript**. This was a key distinction from other languages we’ve studied.

The session then moved on to the topic of **callback functions** and a detailed overview of **JavaScript event handling**. She explained common event handlers like `onload`, `onunload`, `onmouseover`, `onmouseout`, `onsubmit`, and `onclick`. The day's theory concluded with a discussion on **client-side form validation** using JavaScript, complete with an example.

The day ended with a new **JavaScript lab assignment**, which you can find in the `Assignments` folder. It was a busy and productive day, full of essential programming concepts.
