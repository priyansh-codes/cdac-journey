### Morning Sessions: A Focused C++ Theory

Our morning started a bit differently today, as Anu ma'am's theory session was cancelled. We were directly called for the C++ lecture with Yogita ma'am.

She continued from where we left off with **arrays**. We learned about how values are passed to an array as a reference and how to find a specific element within an array. She gave us a detailed breakdown of **single-dimensional arrays** and then moved on to **character arrays and special character arrays**. A key part of the lecture involved calculating the memory address of an element in 1D, 2D, and 3D arrays using both the **row-major and column-major** methods.

We also learned about **sentinel values**, which are used to indicate the end of data in an array. She introduced the `getline` function from the string library and explained the basics of **structures (`struct`)**. She tasked us with a small research assignment on **bit padding** and showed us how to visualize structure members. She also reminded us that a `struct` definition must end with a semicolon.

### Afternoon: Labs and Doubt Solving

After a short break, we went into the lab sessions. The first lab was dedicated to **Web Tech**, where we were given assignments on **JavaScript**. These assignments are available in the `Assignments` folder.

The second lab session was for **C++**. Instead of new assignments, we were encouraged to work on our previously unsolved problems and ask any doubts we had. This was a great opportunity to get clarity on some of the more complex topics we've covered so far.

Overall, it was a day focused on solidifying our understanding of C++ concepts, particularly with arrays and structs.
